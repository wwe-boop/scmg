## 1. Diagnosis: why the counterfactual pointing token works and the yield / semantic attempts did not

What the working recipe actually does (`ctl_cf_p`, losses.py): with probability p a kept POINT token gets **another sample's target**; the segment [a − 0.3, b + 0.3] is **removed from the flow (imitation) loss**; only the **ray loss on the decoded apex frame** (a smooth geometric loss toward the swapped target) supervises it. Result: STG gain +0.147 with the shuffle follow on a bitwise-frozen base (lane 4), +0.15 with tok10ab. The plain token (same route, flow loss on GT) was not followed in round 1.

Three conditions emerge; every failure violates at least one:

| condition | pointing (works) | floor / L1-style YIELD token (rows 13, 16) | semantic head, sem_time, interval tokens (rows 2, 11, 22–25) | count token (row 26) | rest outpainting / mask-cond insertion (rows 15, 30, 31) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C1 Non-redundancy.** The event must carry information about the target that the other conditions (audio, text, history) do not: if I(target; token | audio, text, h) = 0 the flow-matching optimum ignores it | counterfactual swap makes the target depend on the token alone | token placed at the speech offset of GT tails: the audio (silence / "ended" state) already predicts the tail → token redundant → ignored (41.7 vs 40.3 %) | class labels are a deterministic function of the text; stroke timing is not predictable from text (AUC 0.526 < shuffle 0.571): the token adds nothing the flow loss can use | counterfactual counts existed, but see C2 | n/a (inference-time insertion) |
| **C2 An outcome loss in decoded space that is smooth and low-variance** | ray loss on one apex frame, cosine of two directions | none: only imitation of GT tails | none that measures "semantic gesture realised"; pair_win recogniser is excluded from training by rule | soft-count sigmoid on 16 finger joints: grad norm ≈ 9, wrecked FGD (2.2–2.4) and pointing | no training at all; the bridge into a hard known latent is never seen in training → v3 29–41 % |
| **C3 No conflicting imitation on the event segment** | flow loss removed on [a − 0.3, b + 0.3] | flow loss on GT tails (which often do *not* retract: humans stay up; yield3d GT 6.8 %) → the token is taught the human non-yield | flow loss on GT semantic segments: the model is rewarded for matching the human's (unpredictable) timing, not for using the label | flow loss kept | n/a |

Two further observations: (i) module 10b (longer ctl training) raised STG to +0.15, so the control modules on a frozen base have spare capacity when the signal is identifiable; (ii) abruptness came only from inserting *targets the generator never produced itself* (hard latents, pinning); when the generator produces the whole segment under a smooth loss (pointing), ramp jerk was fine (arm ramp jerk ≤ tok). So a network uses an interaction event iff the event is non-redundant with its other inputs, its intended outcome is a smooth decoded-space loss, and imitation is lifted where the event rules. Both proposals below are built on exactly that triple and on the existing `ctl` / `ctl_flow` routes with `--train-only ctl_` (frozen base, no-token path bitwise = base).

**Caveat on L1 (training now).** As registered (L1 addendum 12:3x) the YIELD token sits at the speech offset of train tails with GT motion as the target, i.e. it is the floor-token construction (C1 and C3 violated). The ON / OFF / LATE check will tell; the design below is what to run if L1 reads "token ignored" — or beside it.

## 2. Proposals (ranked)

### P1 (rank 1, interruption): counterfactual YIELD token with a kinematic settle loss ("yield_cf")

**Architecture / loss.** Token type YIELD through the existing `ctl` (planner text stream) + `ctl_flow` (time-local AdaLN) routes, zero-init, trained with `--train-only ctl_` on enc-s0 (frozen; sibag recipe, self_force_scope encoders kept, 2000 steps, lr 1e-4, as lane 4 / module 10b). Training construction, per sample with probability p_cf (0.5):
1. **Placement inside speech** at a stream-grid time t_y with ≥ 2.5 s of speech remaining; the audio and text inputs are left **unchanged** (the speaker keeps talking) — this is the counterfactual: nothing but the token announces a yield (C1). With probability 0.5 the audio after t_y is replaced by digital silence (the deployment input) so both regimes are seen.
2. **Imitation lifted** on [t_y − 0.2, t_y + T], T = 2.0 s (C3), exactly as `ins_cf` does for pointing.
3. **Settle loss on the decoded frames** of that span (C2), all terms smooth, in metres / seconds / rad·s⁻²:
   - quiet: hinge Σ_t relu(s(t) − v_q)·w(t) with s(t) the larger wrist speed and w(t) a raised-cosine weight that starts at t_y + τ and is 1 from t_y + τ + 0.5 s; v_q and the delay τ are **drawn per sample from the half-A human tail statistics** (quiet-speed percentile and time-to-quiet distributions), so the model learns a distribution of settle times, not one deadline;
   - no new stroke: hinge relu(s(t) − v_s) for t ≥ t_y + τ + 0.5 (v_s = half-A stroke threshold);
   - end pose in the human support: soft nearest-neighbour distance of the final 0.5 s mean 12-D wrist / elbow pose to the half-A end-pose set, d_knn = softmin over k = 5 (temperature 0.05 m), hinge above D90 — pulls into the *broad* human support, not to the median rest pose;
   - smoothness: hinge on arm-joint second differences above the train a95 table over the whole span (prevents the pinning jerk of rows 15 / 30 at its source: the generator must produce the bridge itself).
   Weights: quiet 1, stroke 1, end pose 1, smooth 0.3 (units already comparable: m/s, m, rad/s² normalised by a95); overall w_yield 3 (the module-7 weight scale). Non-counterfactual samples: tails of half A with YIELD at the offset keep the flow loss (positive examples) — these are the L1 data; they are a minority and cannot carry the token alone (row 16).
4. **Timing identifiability** (the LATE check made trainable): with probability 0.2 the token is placed but the settle span is shifted by +1.0 s, so the loss rewards settling *at the token time*, not merely "after speech".

**Inference.** `--yield-token` at the interruption frame (as L1), digital silence after it, then the deployed chain (3c-4b + ISC) as a supporting component; no post-processor.

**Why it avoids the failures.** C1: the token is the only yield cue in half the samples (speech continues). C2: four smooth hinges on decoded kinematics, same style as the ray loss (the count loss failed on per-joint sigmoids, not on hinges over wrist kinematics). C3: imitation lifted, so the GT humans who do not retract no longer teach non-yield. Abruptness: the bridge is generated, not inserted, and a95 smoothness is in the loss. Frozen base: the none path is bitwise enc-s0 (checked as lane 4 did), so no EX002 table re-runs.

**Closest prior / delta.** MIRA (hold at barge-in, no retraction, latency-only eval); DyaPlex / StreamAvatar / AVTR-1 (talk–listen state switching learned from dyadic data); our own floor token and L1 (plain token on tails); hindsight experience replay (Andrychowicz 2017: relabel the goal to what happened) and counterfactual data augmentation. Delta: the event is trained as a *prescribed* goal that did **not** happen in the data (the inverse of hindsight relabelling), with imitation lifted and a differentiable settle objective whose parameters are sampled from human post-speech statistics — no dyadic data needed, and the yield behaviour is a property of the generator, not a filter.

**Grounding.** Flow matching learns E[v | conditions]; a conditioning variable with zero conditional mutual information to the target receives zero gradient at the optimum (C1 formalised). The counterfactual construction sets I(settle outcome; YIELD | audio, text, h) > 0 by design, and the hinge losses are the gradient path to it. The settle statistics drawn from half A make the trained behaviour a sample from the human settle distribution rather than a point target — the same logic by which RKD is read against half B.

### P2 (rank 2, semantics): counterfactual exemplar tokens with stroke lead ("sem_cf")

**Architecture / loss.** Token type SEM carrying, as its value vector, a 16-D **kinematic descriptor** d of a human gesture exemplar (train split: wrist-path amplitude, hand, mean direction of the stroke in the shoulder frame, stroke duration, peak speed, end height; computed by the same deterministic function on GT and on generations). Routes and freeze as P1. Training, p_cf = 0.5: a kept BEAT2 semantic segment gets **another segment's descriptor** (swap across the batch); imitation lifted on [a − 0.3, b + 0.3]; the loss is the squared error between the descriptor of the *decoded* span and the given d (smooth: all descriptor components are differentiable functions of wrist trajectories; direction via cosine). Token time = **word onset − 0.2 s** (the stroke-lead finding, 76–79 % of strokes lead by 0.19–0.23 s), legal under a declared TTS-sync protocol row where the robot's TTS timestamps are known ahead (psync stays the main row and is evaluated too). Inference: descriptor from a retrieval bank keyed by lexicon / LLM intent (the existing `ret` bank machinery, row 12), or oracle (GT descriptor of the dev segment) as the upper bound.

**Why it avoids the failures.** C1: the descriptor is not a function of the text (swapped). C2: a smooth decoded-space loss on wrist kinematics, not a recogniser and not a hard latent. C3: imitation lifted. Timing: given by the TTS clock, not predicted (the unpredictability finding is respected). Row 31's edge jerk (hard latent insertion) is avoided because the generator realises the exemplar's *descriptors*, not its frames.

**Closest prior / delta.** Semantic Gesticulator (SIGGRAPH 2024), RAG-Gesture (CVPR 2025), GestureHYDRA — retrieval / exemplar conditioning in offline avatar models; InteractGesture (inference-time guidance, not causal). Delta: causal streaming, frozen base, counterfactual training of a descriptor-valued token so that it is provably non-redundant, stroke-lead timing from TTS.

**Pre-registration draft.** Primary (ours, validated first per the audit: GT = max, mismatched ≈ chance): descriptor realisation error given vs none, with the shuffle follow (swap descriptors: the output must follow the given one); secondary: pair_win full − no-text ≥ +0.10 with CI (the §7.45 bar; current +0.056), and pair_win(oracle) reported as the ceiling; side: FGD v2 (i) / (ii) on the sparse plan, liveliness, executability, none path bitwise. Public protocol: none exists for semantics (SRGR invalid on G1); stated. Controls: plain descriptor token with flow loss (C3 ablation); token at word onset (no lead) and at +0.2 s (late) — the lead must matter; retrieval vs oracle.

**Quick lane (≤ 5 min, 5090).** 2000 steps ctl_ only; 10 dev clips, offline, seed 0: descriptor error given vs none vs swapped. Kill: given not below none by ≥ 30 % of none's error, or the swapped run not following the swapped descriptor.

**Cost.** Quick 0.1 GPU-h; full 3 seeds × offline / psync / TTS-sync ≈ 6 GPU-h. **No base retrain** (frozen-base add-on); the TTS-sync row is a protocol addition, declared.

**Risk.** pair_win may not move even if descriptors are realised (the recogniser measures class timing, not kinematics); then the result is "controllable semantic kinematics", not "better semantics" — stated in advance.

## 3. Framing paragraph

Inside the deployment-faithful streaming framework (causal inputs incl. incremental TTS text, chunk planner + local flow, certified projection as a supporting layer), the method innovation is **counterfactual event conditioning**: interaction events — a barge-in, a command, a semantic intent — are injected as tokens whose training targets are *prescribed outcomes that did not occur in the data*, with imitation lifted on the event span and a smooth decoded-space outcome loss (ray direction, settle kinematics drawn from human post-speech statistics, gesture descriptors) as the only supervision. The identifiability argument (a condition with zero conditional information is ignored by flow matching; the counterfactual construction makes it informative and the outcome loss makes it gradient-accessible) explains both the earlier failures (floor token, semantic head, plain tokens) and the pointing success, and P1 extends it from "where to point" to "when and how to yield" without dyadic data and without a post-processor; the same generator then produces pointing, yielding and (P2) semantic exemplars from one token mechanism. Tables implied: (T1) token-use ON / OFF / LATE for each event type; (T2) interruption benchmark rows — in-network yield_cf vs post-processing R0causal / Funnel (+ ISC) vs B3, all through the same chain, with v3 / unsafe / RKD-post and the yield components; (T3) control: STG, descriptor realisation, pair_win, (i) / (ii), liveliness; (T4) ablations of the three conditions (swap off, flow loss kept, outcome loss off). Unproven today: L1's verdict is pending; no validated yield metric exists (so T2 reports components, never "yields like a human"); P2's effect on pair_win is uncertain; everything is EX002 dev until the protocol freeze.
