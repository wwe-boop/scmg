## 1. Diagnosis of P1

**The gate failed, and the outcome loss was mis-specified; the mechanism itself worked.**

1. *The token is used — first time for an in-network yield.* Wrist speed in 0.5 s bins after t: ON 0.162 / 0.074 / 0.063 / 0.070 vs OFF 0.286 / 0.194 / 0.147 / 0.107 (−43 %, −62 %, −57 %, −35 %); LATE equals OFF for 1 s and then drops (0.102 / 0.072) — the effect follows the token time. Withdrawal ON 0.829 vs OFF 0.732 vs LATE 0.683. The floor token (row 13) and L1 never did this (L1 ON only adds abruptness: v3 41.5 %). So conditions C1–C3 of the diagnosis are confirmed on the interruption side.
2. *The primary measures saturated.* Activity and v4 latency were calibrated on half-A humans (v75 0.397 m/s), but the base's own continuation is quieter than that (U mean speed 0.26–0.27 m/s, U activity 0.829, first quiet run at frame 0). A measure that the unmodified continuation already passes cannot show an ON − OFF difference; "+2.4 pp" is a ceiling effect, not an effect size. Only withdrawal discriminates (U 0.317 vs 0.68–0.83). The kill was therefore registered on a blind instrument; the registered verdict stands as a kill, but it is a gate failure.
3. *Why RKD and the end pose got worse (method defect).* Human post-speech tails are not quiet (half-A v75 0.397 m/s; GT yield3d 6.8 %): humans withdraw while still moving, and they settle into a broad set of poses. The settle loss was a set of **per-sample hinges**, and a hinge is satisfied by the cheapest solution: stop within τ (whose half-A time-to-quiet distribution has a short median — L2d found 0.5 s at the floor) and stay still. The model learned "brake and freeze" (speed 0.06–0.07 m/s for 1.5 s), which is far from the human post-speech distribution → RKD-post 0.503 vs OFF 0.413 (note: still better than R0causal raw 0.536 on the same 41, so the ON row is not a disaster; it is a different point on the withdrawal–likeness trade-off). The end-pose k-NN hinge is only active above D90 and the freeze leaves the arm mid-gesture, so the 3-NN distance moved from 0.103 to 0.118 (the term fired too late and too weakly against the quiet hinge). In short: a point / threshold outcome collapses a *distributional* behaviour to its minimal-effort mode. The pointing token did not suffer from this because its outcome (ray direction) *is* a point.

Consequences for the next lanes: (a) the primary measures must be paired contrasts that cannot be passed by the unmodified continuation; (b) the outcome loss must match a distribution of human post-speech kinematics, not a quiet threshold; (c) the RKD comparison must be read at matched withdrawal (Pareto), since post-processors buy withdrawal with RKD too (R0causal raw 0.536 at ~1.0 withdrawal).

## 2. Next lanes (ranked)

### Non-saturating primary measures (fixed here, before any run; used by both lanes)
All on the same clip, same per_clip seed, raw chain, window W = [t + 0.5, t + 2.0 s]; U = the model's own uninterrupted continuation; OFF = silence without token; LATE = token at t + 1.0 s.
- **M-eff (token effect size).** For descriptor vector D(·) = (mean wrist speed in W, wrist path length in W, descent = mean wrist height drop between [t, t + 0.3] and the last 0.5 s, end-pose 12-D), E = ‖D(ON) − D(OFF)‖_Σ / ‖D(OFF_s) − D(OFF_s')‖_Σ, Mahalanobis with Σ from the OFF rows, denominator = the inference-seed-to-seed distance of OFF (seeds 0 vs 1 on the same clips). Cannot saturate: it is a ratio of a paired contrast to seed noise, with no threshold; the unmodified continuation gives E ≈ 1 by construction. Gate: median E ≥ 3 and the descent component of ON − OFF negative (arms go down, not up) with CI excluding 0.
- **M-time (timing follows the token).** Divergence onset o(X) = first 0.1 s bin where the mean wrist displacement between X and OFF exceeds 2 cm (the existing "react" rule). Gate: median [o(LATE) − o(ON)] ≥ 0.5 s. Cannot saturate at the ceiling: it is a difference of two onsets; it can only fail (both immediate or both absent), which is the intended kill.
- **M-pareto (human likeness at matched withdrawal).** RKD-post (half B) of the token row vs the post-processor whose withdrawal is closest from below and above (R0causal / Funnel / B3 on the same base and chain); gate for the full lane: RKD-post ≤ that of the post-processor with withdrawal ≥ the token row's − 10 pp, CI excluding 0. Report RKD without the end-height descriptor beside it (audit rule) and the end-pose 3-NN distance.
- Side (unchanged): v3 ≤ 10 % after 3c-4b + ISC (abruptness only), unsafe ≤ 1 %, none path bitwise = enc-s0, uninterrupted psync FGD v2 in the enc seed range, liveliness, reaction latency.

### Lane A (rank 1): P1b-SW — distribution-matched settle via sliced Wasserstein on post-token descriptors
**Mechanism.** Same token, routes, counterfactual placement in continuing speech (p 0.5, half with digital silence after t), imitation lifted on [t − 0.2, t + 2.0], `--train-only ctl_` on frozen enc-s0, 2000 steps. The outcome loss is replaced: for the B_cf counterfactual windows in a batch (accumulate over 4 micro-batches so B_cf ≥ 32), compute a differentiable descriptor set d(x) ∈ R^8 on the decoded span — wrist speed means in three bins ([t, t+0.5], [t+0.5, t+1.2], [t+1.2, t+2.0]), path length, descent, end height (two wrists), end-pose distance to the pose at t — and minimise the **sliced Wasserstein-2 distance** (64 random projections, resampled per step) between {d(x_i)} and a bank of half-A human post-speech descriptors computed by the same function at the speech offset (bank built once, train split, A half only). Add the a95 smoothness hinge over the span (kept; it is a physical bound, not a behaviour target). No quiet hinge, no end-pose hinge. The LATE-shift identifiability term is kept (p 0.2: the span and the bank alignment shift with the token). Weight w_yield 3; the SW term is in standardised descriptor units.
**Why it passes where P1 failed.** SW matching is minimised only when the generated post-token descriptors have the *spread* of human tails (fast settlers, slow settlers, arms that stay up), so the minimal-effort freeze is penalised (its speed bins and end heights form a degenerate cluster far from the bank). Descent / end height in the bank carry the withdrawal; the three speed bins carry a human-like deceleration profile instead of an immediate stop. Expected movement: ON speed bins toward the human ones (not 0.07), end-pose 3-NN distance down, RKD-post toward B3's while withdrawal stays above OFF.
**Closest prior / delta.** Distribution-matching motion losses (MMD / Wasserstein on motion statistics in style transfer and physics-based motion priors, e.g. AMP's discriminator as a distribution matcher; sliced-Wasserstein generative training, Deshpande 2018); our L2c/L2d post-processing sampled human end poses. Delta: the distribution target is attached to a counterfactually placed event token inside a frozen causal generator, so the *generator* acquires a human-like yield regime that is switchable at a time of our choosing; no dyadic data, no post-processor.
**Goodhart disclosure.** The loss descriptors overlap in spirit with RKD's five (both are kinematics of the post-speech window); they are computed on half A and RKD is scored on half B with its own descriptor set; v3, withdrawal, M-eff / M-time, FGD v2 and the blind render review are independent reads. Stated in the criteria file before the run.
**Pre-registration draft.** Quick-lane kills (41 interruptions, raw): M-eff median < 3, or o(LATE) − o(ON) < 0.3 s, or RKD-post(ON) > RKD-post(OFF) + 0.05 (the P1 cost was +0.09; the lane must at least halve it), or raw v3 > 25 %. Full lane (476 × 3 training seeds, ISC chain): M-eff ≥ 3, M-time ≥ 0.5 s, M-pareto, side bundle; controls: P1 (hinge) as the ablation of the loss form; SW without the counterfactual placement (bank matched on real tails only; expected: token ignored, as L1); SW with the flow loss kept (C3 ablation); mismatched bank (descriptors from in-speech windows: RKD must be worse — shows the loss is not just "move less").
**Quick lane (≤ 5 min).** 2000 steps ctl_-only on one 5090 (~4 min), 41 interruptions ON / OFF / LATE raw: M-eff, M-time, speed bins, RKD-post, end-pose distance, v3. Why the kills cannot saturate: M-eff is a ratio to seed noise, M-time a difference of onsets, the RKD kill is paired ON − OFF on the same clips.
**Cost.** 0.1 GPU-h quick; ≈ 4 GPU-h full + ISC CPU. **Frozen-base add-on** (no retrain; none path bitwise).

### Lane B (rank 2): P1b-tail-regime — token-switched imitation of relocated human tails with a bridge-consistency term
**Mechanism.** Use the half-A tails as *imitation* data but make the token non-redundant by relocation: the tail's latents (from its speech offset onward, 2.0 s) become the flow target starting at a counterfactual token time t inside another clip's speech, with the audio / text of the host clip unchanged (so only the token predicts the tail-like motion). To avoid the row-31 edge jerk, the first 0.4 s after t are not imitated (lifted) and are supervised only by the a95 smoothness hinge and a continuity term on the decoded wrist position / velocity at t + 0.4 toward the tail's start; the tail's root-relative pose is offset-aligned to the host pose at t (wrist-frame alignment, as the L2c end-pose sampling did) so the bridge distance is small. `--train-only ctl_` on frozen enc-s0, 2000 steps; LATE-shift term kept.
**Why it should pass.** It is the direct way to give the token the human post-speech *distribution* (full trajectories, not descriptors), with the two known failure causes removed: redundancy (relocation inside speech) and bridge jerk (lifted first 0.4 s + continuity). Risk: 78 matched tails are few (the other ~292 retargetable ones are not in data-v36_1); a 2000-step ctl_-only fit may overfit — the mismatched-host control catches memorisation.
**Closest prior / delta.** Motion in-betweening with target clips; our L1 (tails at the true offset, redundant). Delta: relocation of real tails behind a counterfactual token inside speech, with a lifted bridge.
**Pre-registration / quick lane / kills.** Identical measures and kills as Lane A; extra control: host = a different clip at inference (the token must still trigger a tail-like regime). **Cost** as Lane A; **frozen-base add-on**. Rank 2 because of the data count and because trajectory imitation re-introduces the "humans don't always yield" content that lifted imitation was meant to remove (the bank is all tails, so withdrawal is only as frequent as in humans: ~0.80 under v4 i1).

## 3. Does the story hold, and what is the minimum winning evidence

The story "counterfactual event conditioning" **holds on the mechanism** and is now *supported* by the yield side for the first time: a yield token that is non-redundant (placed inside speech), supervised by a decoded-space outcome with imitation lifted, is used by a frozen base and acts at the token time (speed −43 to −62 %, LATE follows). What failed is the *form of the outcome*: a threshold outcome collapses a distributional behaviour to its cheapest mode; the paper's method statement should therefore be "counterfactual event tokens with outcome losses matched to the event's nature: a geometric point loss for commands, a distribution-matching loss for yielding". That is a sharper, testable claim than the original one, and P1 vs P1b-SW is its ablation.

Minimum winning evidence (dev, pre-registered, then one test run after the freeze):
1. Token use that cannot be a ceiling effect: M-eff ≥ 3 and M-time ≥ 0.5 s on 476 interruptions × 3 training seeds, CI excluding the gate.
2. Through the deployed chain with ISC: v3 ≤ 10 %, unsafe ≤ 1 %, none path bitwise = base, FGD v2 and liveliness unchanged.
3. Pareto over post-processing on the same base and chain: at withdrawal within 10 pp of R0causal + ISC, RKD-post lower with CI excluding 0 (or: at B3's RKD, withdrawal higher). Reported with the components and RKD-without-end-height; never as "yields like a human".
4. The three-condition ablation (swap off / flow kept / outcome off) and the loss-form ablation (hinge vs SW) showing each part is necessary.
5. A blind render review (main agent, EVAL_PROTOCOL §4) finding no freeze / hold in the token rows.
If 1–2 pass and 3 fails, the honest result is "in-network, token-timed yielding at the post-processors' likeness level" — a capability, not a win over post-processing; the user decides whether that carries the interaction chapter.
