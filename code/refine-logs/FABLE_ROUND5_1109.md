## 1. Diagnosis: why in-network tokens under-realise and brake

Pattern across pointing, semantics and yield: the token *is followed* (direction, descriptor, timing) but the *magnitude* is a fraction of the command (amplitude ~50 %, peak speed ~33 %), and whenever the outcome admits a low-energy solution the model takes it (brake). Four causes, all visible in how the ctl_ losses are computed and where the modules sit:

1. **The outcome is scored on a conditional-mean estimate, not on a sample.** The ctl losses (ray, descriptors, settle) are evaluated on the decoded one-step denoised estimate x̂₁(x_τ) of the training chunk. At the noise levels where the loss is active this estimate is close to E[x₁ | conditions]: smooth and low-amplitude by construction. The adapter therefore learns the smallest shift of the *mean* that satisfies the loss; the actual 10-step CFG-2.0 sample at inference inherits the base's dynamics, and the shift shows up as half-amplitude. Keyframes do not have this problem: they set the pose, not a mean.
2. **A frozen base with zero-init AdaLN adapters can re-scale and shift features, not re-write the velocity field.** `ctl_flow` adds to the AdaLN condition of the Local Flow; `ctl` enters the planner's text stream. Both modulate; neither adds the coordinated multi-joint, multi-chunk excursion that a legible gesture needs. Across the 320 ms chunk boundary the history is self-generated by the frozen base, so each new chunk regresses toward the base's typical motion; the token's influence must be re-asserted every chunk and decays (the 5b blend diagnostic showed given − none decaying by +1.0 s).
3. **Outcome losses are under-determined and asymmetric.** A 16-D descriptor or a 3-term settle loss constrains a span of ~100 frames × 34 joints; the minimum-norm solution the adapter finds is the cheapest one, and for speed-type terms cheap = slower (the base's own low-speed mode is nearby; raising speed requires fighting the prior). Lifted imitation removes the signal that would otherwise fix the amplitude distribution. Hence "brake", for hinges and for sliced-Wasserstein alike — the SW target was right, but the adapter could not reach the fast members of the bank, and the loss settled on matching the slow ones.
4. **Signal starvation.** ~5 effective counterfactual samples per batch (early placements dropped for lack of 4 history chunks) is a 10× weaker gradient than the pointing lanes had (every kept POINT token).

Why joint-space keyframes work: the target is fully determined (34 joints at a time), the dynamics are supplied explicitly (minimum-jerk ramps, release search), and nothing is asked of the frozen prior. The in-network version must therefore (a) specify a fully determined target, (b) score the outcome on the generator's *own multi-step rollout* across chunk boundaries, (c) give the adapter enough capacity and signal, and (d) still let the generator produce the bridge itself (the hard-paste routes of rows 30 / 31 are what produced v3 29 % and +18 FGD).

## 2. Designs (ranked)

### A (rank 1): keyframe-conditioned generation with a rollout-scored outcome — "hit the keyframe with your own bridge"
**Mechanism.**
- *Token value* = a fully determined joint-space keyframe: the commanded arm's 7 joints (+ the 8 finger joints for present / count) and the keyframe time t_k, Fourier-featured through the existing `ctl` / `ctl_flow` routes (value vector extended; type KEY). The keyframes are **produced by the existing IK pipeline used as a label generator** (point toward a train target, present, SELF, YOU, NUMBER) at counterfactual times inside speech of train items — the pipeline moves from inference post-processing to training supervision; at inference only the token is given.
- *Rollout-scored loss* (the fix for cause 1): for the chunk(s) containing t_k, the adapter is trained through a short **with-gradient sampler rollout**: 4 Euler steps with CFG 2.0 (the inference sampler at reduced steps), decoded by the frozen VAE, with the preceding 2 chunks generated no-grad by the current model (the `self_force` machinery, grad enabled only on the final chunk). Losses on the decoded rollout: L_key = ‖q(t_k) − q_key‖² on the commanded joints; L_hold = the same over [t_k, t_k + 0.3 s]; L_bridge = a95 acceleration hinge on the commanded arm over [t_k − 0.6, t_k + 0.6]; imitation lifted on [t_k − 0.6, t_k + 0.8] as in ctl_cf. Counterfactual by construction (the keyframe was never in the data). Weight w_key 3, w_hold 1, w_bridge 0.3.
- *Signal* (cause 4): keyframes placed only where ≥ 4 history chunks exist, one per sample (every sample carries one), so the effective count is the batch size, not 5.
- *Capacity* (cause 2): stage 1 adapter-only (`--train-only ctl_`, frozen base, none path bitwise). Stage 2, only if stage 1 passes the follow check but realises < 70 %: **token-gated LoRA** on the Local Flow's attention / MLP (rank 8), whose delta is multiplied by the module-6 window w_k(t) (1 on [t_k − 0.6, t_k + 0.8], cosine ramps, 0 elsewhere), so the none path and every frame outside a command window stay **bitwise** the base while inside the window the velocity field itself can change. This is what makes "the generator realises the gesture" true rather than "the adapter nudges the mean".
**Why it avoids the earlier failures.** No latent is pasted (rows 30 / 31 / 46): the generator produces the bridge and the apex under a smoothness hinge, so the off-manifold cusp and the +18 FGD inpainting cost have no source. The target is fully determined (cause 3), scored on a sample (cause 1), with full-batch signal (cause 4) and, in stage 2, with local capacity (cause 2). Quality outside windows is protected by construction (gated LoRA) — the same construction that made (i) hold for module 6.
**Closest prior / delta.** Keyframe / spatial-constraint conditioned motion diffusion: OmniControl (ICLR 2024, spatial guidance + realism guidance), CondMDI (SIGGRAPH 2024, flexible in-betweening with masked conditioning), GMD (ICCV 2023), MotionLCM control; reward fine-tuning of diffusion through the sampler: DRaFT (Clark et al. 2023), AlignProp (Prabhudesai 2023). Delta: a *causal streaming* generator with a frozen base, keyframes as **counterfactual event tokens** (never in the data, supplied by a geometric solver at training time), the outcome scored through the chunked sampler across self-generated history, and window-gated LoRA so the no-event path is bitwise unchanged. None of the priors is causal / chunked or distils a solver's keyframes into tokens.
**Pre-registration draft (dev, EX002, frozen enc-s0; plans 1112 dense + sparse; offline, psync; per_clip 0–2).**
- Primary, realisation (ours, validated first: GT-own 0, mismatched / swapped ≈ the none level): R = 1 − d(ON) / d(OFF), d = joint-space distance to the keyframe at t_k on the commanded joints; gate median R ≥ 0.7; amplitude ratio (excursion of the commanded wrist from its pose at t_k − 0.6 s, ON vs the keyframe's own excursion) ≥ 0.75; v_peak ratio ≥ 0.6; swap follow: d to the given keyframe < d to the swapped one with CI excluding 0.
- Primary, public protocol: STG(given) − STG(none) ≥ +0.10 with the shuffle follow and the D3 cheat controls (point keyframes); canonical explicit-semantic legibility (SELF / YOU / NUMBER / BIG / SMALL detectors as in the canonical table) ≥ the IK-keyframe row − 10 pp.
- Side (audit): FGD v2 (i) / (ii) on the sparse plan ≤ C + δ (outside windows bitwise in stage 2); liveliness guard; executability after 3c-4b 100 %; bridge acceleration p95 vs the IK-keyframe ramps and vs none; release continuity median + p95 + max; none path bitwise = enc-s0 (stage 1 and 2).
- Controls: IK keyframe insertion (the current canonical path, upper reference); stage-1 adapter-only vs stage-2 gated LoRA; rollout-scored vs one-step-scored loss (the cause-1 ablation, same keyframes); counterfactual off (keyframes only where GT already has a deictic event — expected weaker follow); window gate off (expected: (i) fails as lane 4 did).

### B (rank 2): rollout-scored training for the existing event tokens (descriptor / settle) — the cause-1 test on its own
**Mechanism.** Keep sem_cf and yield_cf exactly as they are (tokens, counterfactual placement, lifted imitation, losses), and change only *where the loss is evaluated*: on the decoded 4-step CFG rollout of the chunk(s) containing the event (grad through the sampler, history no-grad by self-forcing), instead of the one-step estimate. For yield_cf, also fix the signal starvation (placements only where history exists; one event per sample) and use the v2 tail bank (the 108 frozen tails removed) for the SW target.
**Why.** Isolates cause 1 with the smallest code change (the self_force path already produces rollouts; enabling gradients on the last chunk is the delta). Prediction from the diagnosis: amplitude / v_peak ratios rise from 0.5 / 0.33 toward 0.75 / 0.6 for sem_cf; for yield, the SW loss can now reach the fast bank members (speed bins toward 0.24 / 0.20 / 0.16), so RKD ON − OFF should fall below +0.05. If neither moves, cause 1 is not the bottleneck and only A's capacity / target changes remain.
**Closest prior / delta.** DRaFT / AlignProp (reward fine-tuning through the sampler, whole model, image diffusion); delta: adapter-only, causal chunked rollout with self-generated history, kinematic decoded-space outcomes, frozen base.
**Pre-registration / quick lane.** Same measures as the sem_cf and yield_cf lanes (E with validity rows; M-eff / M-time / RKD at matched withdrawal; side gates), compared at **matched wall-clock training budget** against the one-step-scored models. Quick lane: 600 steps rollout-scored vs 600 steps one-step-scored (same seed, same 10 recordings), offline: amplitude ratio, v_peak ratio, E (sem); speed bins and RKD ON − OFF (yield). Kills (relative to the matched one-step model, cannot saturate): amplitude ratio not ≥ 1.3× the one-step model's; for yield, RKD ON − OFF not reduced by ≥ 0.05. **Cost** 0.2 GPU-h quick, ≈ 4 GPU-h full; frozen-base add-on.

## 3. Frank view: which in-network claim is defensible now

**Defensible with the evidence in hand (dev, EX002, frozen base):**
- *Counterfactual event tokens make a frozen causal generator follow events it otherwise ignores* — pointing STG +0.15 with the shuffle follow (tok10ab, all gates); semantic descriptor realisation −44 % with the oracle and swap follow (10 recordings; full run pending); yield token acting at the token time (M-time 1.1 s). This is a **controllability** claim with a measured magnitude and a measured limit: realisation ≈ 50 % amplitude / 33 % peak speed, and a likeness cost on yield. It is in-framework (network + training), it has an identifiability account, and its ablations (plain token vs counterfactual) exist on G1 and EX002.
- *Encoder-only self-forcing* as a training method (3 EX002 seeds, liveliness; G1 unverified) — in-framework, not interaction.

**Not defensible now:** in-network *human-like* yielding (two outcome forms both braked; RKD worse than OFF); a deployable semantic source (≈ 4 % information from text + prosody — the honest statement is that monologue inputs do not predict the gesture descriptor); keyframe-level legibility *from the network* (the canonical table passes only through IK keyframes + finger override; the tokens alone under-realise).

**What the paper can say today, in-framework, without over-claiming:** the event layer is a hybrid — counterfactual tokens give the generator the intent (direction, hand, timing, descriptor; followed with a measured ~50 % realisation) and a joint-space keyframe layer supplies geometric precision, with ablations showing (i) tokens alone under-realise, (ii) keyframes alone on the none stream reach the same geometry (the 2-clip control), (iii) both together pass the gates. If lane A passes, the second clause disappears and "the generator realises keyframe-level gestures from event tokens" becomes the method; if it fails, the defensible in-network innovation is the controllability mechanism, and the user should decide whether that carries the interaction chapter or whether the certified projection + ISC (the only component with a theorem) returns to the front.

Open items that gate any of this: full-dev 3-seed sem_cf rows; the v2 tail reference re-scores for every interruption row (108 frozen tails contaminated the earlier bank statistics and RKD references); G1 check of encoder-only self-forcing; EX002 test runs after the protocol freeze.
